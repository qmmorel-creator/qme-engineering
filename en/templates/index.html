How does one-line entry work?

You type the task name followed, in any order, by a date (12/12, or 2027-01-15), a duration (5d, 2 wk), progress (40%) and its predecessors (after 3, 5SS+2). "milestone" or "0d" creates a milestone, "window 12/10-12/18" a date window, and a line "_Phase_2" opens a section. Dates follow your format, day/month or month/day, chosen in the settings. You can paste several lines at once; everything can be edited in the table.

Which public holidays are included?

They are calculated, not copied, so they stay correct every year. Each plan has its own calendar: England and Wales (8 bank holidays, with substitute days when a holiday falls on a weekend), United States (11 federal holidays, observed on the Friday or Monday), France (11 holidays, with the Alsace-Moselle option) or none. State or regional holidays, one-off holidays decided for a given year, bridge days and closures can be added as periods.

What is the critical path?

It is the sequence of tasks with no float: one day of delay on any of them moves the end of the project. For each task, the planner calculates the early and late dates, total float (delay possible without moving the end) and free float (without delaying the next task).

Can I print or share the plan?

Yes: print in A4 or A3 landscape, with the whole plan fitting the page width (and save as PDF from the print window), or download a PNG image. The people you send it to do not need access.

Does it replace professional project management software?

It covers the essentials of a project plan: dependencies, calendar, critical path, baseline and tracking. It does not manage resources, costs or several people editing at the same time: it is designed for the person who keeps the plan.

Can I switch between English and French?

Yes: the interface language is a setting, and the same key opens the same plans in either language. Your own task names are never translated.

Can I try it before buying?

Where is my data stored?

Online, on the website's hosting (Netlify, European Union region), linked to your key. A copy stays in your browser so you can keep working offline; changes are sent as soon as you are back online. From the settings, you can also download a copy of your plans.

On how many devices can I use it?

On all of yours, with the same key: your plans are the same everywhere. The key is personal: sharing it means sharing your plans.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The template works in any recent browser. On a phone, you can add it to your home screen ("Add to Home Screen" in Safari or Chrome) to open it like an app.

Is it a subscription?

No: a one-time purchase, with no renewal.
