Custom-built, whatever your trade

Hydropower, retail, professional practice, engineering firm, nursery, gym, shop, events, trades, health: each tool below was designed for a specific trade.

Operations · hydropower

Thalvego

The whole valley at a glance: structures, worksites, incidents and reservoirs placed on the real terrain. Each worksite opens in detailed 3D, with its team, its safety and its progress.

  • Scheme indicators at the top of the screen: power, reservoirs, on-call staff
  • Ranked alerts: co-activity, lockouts, overdue worksites
  • Optional modules: weather, resources, risks, simulated generation
  • One shared engine, replicated site by site

Bike shops · e-bikes · cargo bikes

BikePulse Gear

The quote and the invoice are built on the bike, in 3D: click on the part, the job is added, the totals are recalculated.

Nurseries · early childhood

Bricabulle

Nursery management with accurate billing: from the schedule booked by parents to the hours actually attended, all the way to the invoice.

Engineering firm · confidential client

Drawing approvals

Each drawing tracked until it is approved for construction. What could delay the site shows up before it holds up the works.

Project management

Nexora

Tasks, meetings and Gantt charts in one app, with AI assistants that keep tasks up to date.

Health · nutrition

NutriQuest

A meal is described in one sentence, by voice or with a photo: AI finds the foods in the national nutrition table, computes the intake and fills in the spreadsheet. The balance is compared with the energy measured by the watch.

CrossFit gym · competitions

Box ABCD

The competition planned in 3D: lanes and teams, equipment placed then checked, heats and timer on screen, live leaderboard and TV mode for spectators.

Gyms · competitions

WOD Motion

The workout, described in a few lines, becomes a 30-second animated clip with music: movements, reps, clock, heart rate, in the colours of a gym or a competition.

Game shop · game bar

La Taverne des Dés

Till and loyalty, stock and pre-orders, game nights with waiting lists and tournaments: a whole game shop and bar in one tool.

Events · wedding planners

Nozze Maestria

From first contact to the big day: backward schedule, suppliers, guests, quotes, and the whole wedding day on a dial.

Building trades

Tradesman quotes

A complete quote in minutes: standard work items per trade, 5.5, 10 or 20 % VAT, deposit and mandatory notices, A4 preview ready to sign (French market).
