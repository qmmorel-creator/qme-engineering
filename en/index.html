Custom-built, whatever your trade

Hydropower, retail, professional practice, engineering firm, nursery, gym, shop, events, trades, health: each tool below was designed for a specific trade.

Operations · hydropower

Thalvego

The whole valley at a glance: structures, worksites, incidents and reservoirs placed on the real terrain. Each worksite opens in detailed 3D, with its team, its safety and its progress.

  • Scheme indicators at the top of the screen: power, reservoirs, on-call staff
  • Ranked alerts: co-activity, lockouts, overdue worksites
  • Optional modules: weather, resources, risks, simulated generation
  • One shared engine, replicated site by site

Health · nutrition

NutriQuest

A meal is described in one sentence, by voice or with a photo: AI finds the foods in the national nutrition table, computes the intake and fills in the spreadsheet. The balance is compared with the energy measured by the watch.
