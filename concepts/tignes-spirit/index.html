Concept for Tignes Spirit

A Saturday changeover board: one bookings list for all channels, driver runs on a phone or tablet, the workshop queue, pre-arrival sizes and your apartments on one map. Everything can be tailored to the way your team works and built in modules.

QME · Expected gain

What the tool saves you

Around 300 hours saved each seasonclose to two months of admin work, given back to customers, the shop and the workshop.

Time saved

  • Re-keying bookings from email, phone and the website: around 100 hours per season
  • Building the daily driver runs: around 80 hours
  • Chasing sizes and answering "when is my delivery?": around 75 hours
  • Saturday workshop coordination and daily cash-up: around 55 hours

What becomes reliable

  • One bookings list for every channel: no double booking, nothing lost in an inbox
  • Driver runs built from the bookings, grouped by village and time
  • Sizes collected before arrival, so kit is ready on Saturday
  • Damage flagged in the workshop queue before the next rental
  • Cash-up reconciled every evening
